# Seller: Reports

Menu: **Reports** (`/seller/reports`). Pick a report, pick the dates, read it on screen, or press **Download CSV** to open it in Excel or Google Sheets.

- **Sales:** money coming in, by day or month.
- **My products:** Best sellers, Slow sellers, Low stock.
- **Orders:** how many are in each step, how many were cancelled or returned, and how fast you deliver.
- **What I earn:** sales minus system commission minus refunds = what you keep.

Quick dates: Last 7 days, Last 30 days, This month, Last month, This year, or choose From and To. At most 366 days at a time. Low stock shows stock right now, so the dates do not apply.

API: see `report-guide.md`.
