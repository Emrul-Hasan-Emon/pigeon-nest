COMMISSION: WHAT CUSTOMERS AND GUESTS SEE
=========================================

Nothing. System commission is between the platform and the seller.

- The price you see in the cart, at checkout, and on your order is the whole
  price. System commission is never added to it, and it does not change what you pay.
- Your order page, order tracking page and order list never show a system commission or
  the seller's share. The fields do not exist in what is sent to you.
- You cannot reach any system commission address: signed in as a customer, or not signed
  in at all, every system commission address answers 401 or 403.
- If you cancel your order, or a payment on it is reversed, the platform adjusts
  the seller's system commission by itself. You do not need to do anything.

Orders a seller or admin creates for you work the same: the system commission is only
visible to them.

See `../commission-guide.md` for the full picture, and `../admin/commission-flow.md`
and `../seller/commission-flow.md` for the admin and seller views.
