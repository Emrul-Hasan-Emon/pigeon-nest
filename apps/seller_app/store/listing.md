## Name
Pigeon Nest Seller

## Short description (Play) / Subtitle (App Store)
Run your pigeon shop from your phone.

## Full description
Pigeon Nest Seller is the app for sellers on the Pigeon Nest marketplace. Manage your whole shop from your phone, with big buttons and plain words.

Orders
- See new orders, confirm, pack, ship and deliver them
- Move many orders at once
- Record payments with proof, share or print an invoice
- Enter an order for a phone or in-shop customer

Products
- Add birds and supplies with prices, offers, stock and pictures
- Ring number, hatch date, vaccine, father and mother for every bird
- Copy a product, switch products on or off, change many prices at once

Money
- See what you keep, what you owe the marketplace and every charge
- Confirm "I have paid" messages from buyers
- Handle returns and refunds step by step
- Monthly statements and sales, earnings, products and returns reports

Customers
- Answer price offers: accept, counter or reject
- Answer questions and reply to reviews
- See how often your products are saved

Plus a dashboard with sales, refunds and what needs your attention today, a set-up checklist, help pages, and notifications for new orders, questions, offers, payment messages and returns.

You need a Pigeon Nest seller account. New shops can register in the app and are checked before they sell.

## Data safety / privacy answers
- Collected: name, phone, email, shop address and ID pictures (account and verification), order and payment records, product pictures, push token.
- Used for: running the account and the marketplace, notifications. Not sold. Not used for ads.
- Sent over HTTPS only. A seller can ask for the account to be removed (needs a privacy page and a contact; see below).
- Required before submitting: a public privacy policy URL, a support email, and for the App Store a demo seller login for the reviewer.
