## Full description
Pigeon Nest is a marketplace for pigeons, bloodlines and racing homer supplies, sold directly by sellers around the world. Browse a premium catalogue of birds, save your favourites, and order with confidence.

Browse
- Homepage picks, categories, collections and sellers
- Search and filter birds by breed, bloodline, price and more
- A product page with photos, variant picker, breed and bloodline details, and related birds

Buying
- A cart per seller, saved offers you've made, and a guided checkout
- Pay by card or bank/mobile transfer, with proof upload where needed
- Track every order: status, delivery, payment, and updates from the seller

After the sale
- Save birds to your wishlist with live price and stock
- Write a review with photos once your order is delivered
- Ask the seller a question before you buy
- Propose your own price on a bird, and counter-offer with the seller
- Request a return on an eligible order

No account is needed to browse, and guests can check out and track an order by its number and phone. Sign in for your orders, offers, reviews, wishlist and saved addresses.

## Data safety / privacy answers
- Collected: name, phone, email, delivery addresses, order and payment records, review photos, push token.
- Used for: running the account and placing orders, notifications. Not sold. Not used for ads.
- Sent over HTTPS only. A buyer can ask for the account to be removed (needs a privacy page and a contact; see below).
- Required before submitting: a public privacy policy URL, a support email, and for the App Store a demo buyer login (or guest checkout) for the reviewer.
